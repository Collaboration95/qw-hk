# Train

A travel-poster dusk with rails, poles and a low sun. The first press brings a little steam train in from the left pulling one flatcar per letter and a brake van carrying the mark. It brakes with sparks, the couplings bump in a ripple, the mark and letters lift off into the wordmark above, the whistle puffs, and it chugs out to the right. ~2.4 s. Reduced motion and print show the final lockup.

**Use it for** logistics, shipping and freight, travel and mobility, supply chain, delivery and marketplaces that move goods.
**Avoid it for** software with nothing physical to move, high-tech or AI positioning (the steam age reads nostalgic), and very long names (they work but the train gets small).

## Adapt it
- **Brand**: `--field` (the sky; everything in the land is tinted from it), `--brand` (the train, the mark plate), `--brand-2` (brass, lamps). Set them on the `<section>` or once in `theme.css` as `--deck-brand` / `--deck-brand-2`.
- **Mark**: `__MARK__` (use-pack `--mark`): stroke paths in a 24 x 24 box, on the brake van's plate in `--on-brand`.
- **Word**: letters in `--ink-auto`. One flatcar per character up to 12, two per wagon beyond; the train and wordmark shrink to fit (`--rv-fs`).
- **Layout**: `--rv-y` moves the lockup.

## Params
- `--field`: the sky; hills, ground, sun and steam are tinted from it
- `--brand`: the train and the mark's enamel plate
- `--brand-2`: brass and lamps
- `--rv-fs`: wordmark size (shrinks for long names)
- `--rv-y`: the lockup's centre line
- `__MARK__`: stroke paths in a 24 x 24 box, set on the brake van's enamel plate in --on-brand
- `__WORD__`: one flatcar per character up to 12, two per wagon beyond
