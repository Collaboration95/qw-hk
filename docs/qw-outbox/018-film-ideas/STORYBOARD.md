# 018 · Three submission films for Recall Press

Product line: **"Your sources change. Your deck updates itself."**
All three films: ~10 shots, hard cuts between shots, slide-style motion graphics inside each shot,
the light v3 look from `site/v2/src/system.css` (paper `#FFFFFF`, warm wash `#FFFBF8`, ink `#111110`,
quiet `#5B5953`, dim `#6B6862`, one accent red `#C93A1B`, Inter, ease `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`).
Every number below is taken from the project files (README "One revision, two answers",
`home-02-run.html`, `home-04-grid.html`, `docs/submission/description.md`). Nothing is invented.
Honesty carried in every film: the editor approves every change; the sample decks are founder-written;
the figures, dates and sources are real releases.

Real numbers used: 0.0% (14 Oct 2025) → 5.0% (21 Nov 2025) → 4.9% (2 Jan 2026) → 5.3% (10 Feb 2026)
→ 4.6% (14 Apr 2026); services 0.6% → 0.7%; 60 figures (10 indicators × 6 quarters), 57 revised,
18 sentence-breaking; 6 slides in the members' briefing; watched since 20 Oct 2025;
8 claims read on the fresh deck, 8/8 verdicts matched (Job 016).

## Which film I would pick

**Film A, "One revision, two answers."**

1. **It leads with the only thing a competitor can't copy by accident: judgment.** Any tool can
   find-and-replace a number. Shot 4 — one revision, two verdicts, "changing it would introduce an
   error" — is the whole product in one frame, and it matches the README's own lead story.
2. **It fits the owner's taste.** A two-sided tension in the opening minutes (taste rule 2), the real
   artifact on screen (the desk, the run log, the grid — not stock diagrams), proof before claims,
   and the honesty line spoken, not buried.
3. **It is the cheapest to make well.** Shots 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 are re-skins of markup that already
   exists in `site/v2/src/` — the motion graphics are CSS on real UI, which is both on-brand and
   nearly free compared with generated footage (see `qwenwork-out/08-media/media-costs.md`:
   local renders cost 0 credits; generated video costs ~16–28 credits/s).
4. Film B is the strongest runner-up (the run log as plot is very "show the real artifact"), but it
   spends 40 seconds before the two-answers moment. Film C has the best cold open but risks reading
   as a data report before the product appears.

Opening shot of Film A is built as `shot-01.html` in this folder.
