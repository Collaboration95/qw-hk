# Grants check — can a Singapore SME buy Recall Press with government support?

Checked: 2026-11-19. All quotes below were fetched from official pages on that date unless marked otherwise. Verdicts are deliberately conservative.

## Headline finding (verified directly)

**PSG, EDG and MRA ceased on 29 September 2026.** The live successor is the **EDGE Grant** (launched 30 September 2026). Verbatim from the EnterpriseSG PSG page (https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/productivity-solutions-grant, fetched 2026-11-19):

> "EDG, MRA and PSG ceased on 29 September 2026. From 30 September onwards, apply for business grant support under the EDGE Grant."

Ongoing PSG/EDG/MRA projects continue to be processed; no new applications under those schemes are accepted (confirmed in the EDGE FAQ, https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/resources/all-faqs/edge-grant).

## 1. Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG) — EnterpriseSG track CLOSED

- URLs: https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/productivity-solutions-grant · https://grants.gobusiness.gov.sg/support/productivity-solutions-grant · FAQ: https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/resources/all-faqs/productivity-solutions-grant
- Support level (verbatim): "Up to 50% of eligible costs for local SMEs" / "Receive up to $30,000". FAQ: annual grant cap of S$30,000, resetting 1 April–31 March.
- Eligibility (verbatim, PSG page):
  > "Business entity is registered and operating in Singapore"
  > "Company has at least 30% local equity held directly or indirectly by Singaporean(s) and/or Singapore PR(s), determined by the ultimate individual ownership"
  > "Group annual sales turnover not exceeding S$100 million OR group employment size not exceeding 200 employees"
  > "IT solutions or equipment that are purchased, leased, or subscribed to must be used in Singapore."
  > Retrospective applications (payment or deposit before application) "will not be supported".
  > Charities, IPCs, religious entities, VWOs, government agencies and subsidiaries, and societies are not eligible.
- Pre-approval requirement (verbatim): "Get support for sector-specific and generic solutions that are pre-approved by EnterpriseSG and other participating agencies." PSG FAQ: "No, companies can only be supported on pre-scoped packages by pre-approved vendors." Bespoke excluded: "PSG supports businesses in the adoption of pre-scoped IT solutions and/or equipment that are tried-and-tested, and easy to deploy with no customisation."
- Surviving PSG directory (other agencies only — BCA, ECDA, MinLaw, NEA, NParks, SFA): https://grants.gobusiness.gov.sg/support/productivity-solutions-grant/psg-directory — "From 30 Sep 2026, Enterprise Singapore PSG solutions will no longer appear in this directory." Categories seen (e.g. Document Management System, Data and AI-driven Decision Support System for Built Environment) do not include statistics-revision monitoring.
- **Verdict: unlikely.** The EnterpriseSG track is closed; surviving agency lists have no matching category; and Recall Press is not pre-approved (see §5 for why it cannot be yet).

## 2. Enterprise Development Grant (EDG) — CLOSED

- URL: https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/enterprise-development-grant
- Support level (verbatim): "Up to 50% of eligible costs for local SMEs" (sustainability up to 70%).
- Eligibility (verbatim): "Business entity registered and operating in Singapore"; "at least 30% local equity" (same wording as PSG); "Company is financially ready to start and complete the project. Commonly used financial indicators, such as the current ratio, will be used for assessment."
- Qualifying costs (verbatim): "EDG funds qualifying project costs namely third-party consultancy fees, software and equipment, and internal manpower cost."
- Off-the-shelf exclusions (verbatim, Automation category "What is not covered"):
  > "Equipment and software costs that are supported under the Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG)"
  > "Installation of conventional, operational and off-the-shelf solutions, appliances or devices"
  > "Replacement/ addition of solutions with little changes to resource efficiency and no new capabilities developed"
- Process Redesign covers workflow review, "Exploring the use of technology to automate processes", and piloting; projects "must be new, have not commenced, and are not generating any revenue at the point of grant application."
- **Verdict: unlikely** for buying the subscription itself — EDG never funded stand-alone off-the-shelf subscriptions, and it is closed anyway. A genuine transformation project could in principle embed software costs, but a niche monitoring SaaS alone is not such a project.

## 3. EDGE Grant — the CURRENT scheme

- URLs: https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/edge-grant · FAQ: https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/resources/all-faqs/edge-grant · single-function solutions: https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/edge-grant/automation-and-digitalisation/single-function-solutions
- Support level (verbatim, FAQ): "up to 50% support for SMEs and up to 30% support for non-SMEs (up to 70% support for internationalisation and sustainability activities for SMEs and up to 50% support for non-SMEs)".
- Caps (verbatim, FAQ): "annual grant cap of S$100,000 for all eligible activities"; "the company can use up to S$30,000 on single-function digital solutions, integrated enterprise systems and pre-scoped equipment". "Selected single-function digital solutions & integrated enterprise systems: Limited to one application per UEN."
- Eligibility (verbatim, FAQ):
  > "The applicant must be a business entity registered in Singapore with at least 30% Singaporean(s) and/or Singapore PR(s) ownership. Other requirements may apply, depending on the supportable activity."
  > "An SME refers to a company that has a group annual sales turnover of not more than S$100 million, or a group employment size of no more than 200 workers."
  > Non-profits, societies, statutory boards and government subsidiaries are rejected.
- Pre-approval still applies to single-function SaaS (verbatim, FAQ): "For activities with pre-approved vendors, businesses should choose from the list as they have been assessed for their credibility." Single-function solutions are browsed on the IMDA SMEs Go Digital platform (https://smesgodigital.gov.sg — JS-rendered, content not extractable). The only own-vendor route is Integrated ERP: "Use your own vendor if you can't find a suitable pre-approved vendor. Solution should include at least 3 core functions." (Core functions: Asset/Field Service/Financial/Fleet/HR/Manufacturing/Project/Supply Chain/Sales & CRM — no statistics monitoring.)
- Reimbursement basis; no retrospective applications; applicant must apply, pay and claim itself (no vendor submissions).
- **Verdict: unlikely.** Same structural barrier as PSG: the SaaS must be a pre-approved, pre-scoped package in a defined category. Recall Press is neither listed nor listable yet (§5).

## 4. IMDA programmes

- **SMEs Go Digital** (https://www.imda.gov.sg/how-we-can-help/smes-go-digital — page live, body JS-blocked): its funding mechanism is the pre-approved solutions list, now feeding EDGE. It does not subsidise arbitrary non-pre-approved SaaS. **Verdict: unlikely.**
- **CTO-as-a-Service**: platform live at https://services2.imda.gov.sg/CTOaaS (redirects to smesgodigital.gov.sg/web/ on re-open). The IMDA programme page redirected to homepage (blocked/moved). Third-party descriptions (labelled as such: fintechnews.sg, grantla.com snippets) describe digital advisory + curated pre-approved solutions; subsidies flow only via the pre-approved list. **Verdict: unlikely** — advisory may help an SME plan, but does not fund a non-listed subscription.
- **GenAI Sandbox / Sandbox 2.0**: real; official pages exist (https://www.imda.gov.sg/resources/press-releases-factsheets-and-speeches/press-releases/2024/sg-first-genai-sandbox-for-smes and .../factsheets/2024/gen-ai-sandbox-2-0, dated 29 Oct 2024; bodies JS-blocked). Subsidises curated GenAI solutions (marketing/sales/customer-engagement per snippets) — not statistics-revision monitoring. **Verdict: unlikely.**
- **Digital Enterprise Blueprint** (https://www.mddi.gov.sg/digital-enterprise-blueprint/, fetched; updated 31 Aug 2026): a blueprint of partner commitments (e.g. subsidised Microsoft Copilot, DBS Spark GenAI), not a grant for buying arbitrary SaaS. **Verdict: unlikely.**

## 5. Could Recall Press itself become pre-approved? (vendor-side rules)

Verbatim from IMDA's official Pre-Approval guide (https://preapproval-guide.imda.gov.sg/pre-approval-guide/eligibility-criteria, fetched 2026-11-19):

> "ICM Vendor's company must have been incorporated for a minimum of 18 months."
> "At least 5 SMEs who have used the solution for a minimum period of 6 months, and are currently still using the solution." (SMEs must not be subsidiaries or affiliated companies.)
> "At least 5 SME customers have indicated achieving 15% or more productivity gain from using the solution."
> "ICM Vendor must have a positive net equity in the latest financial year." Current ratio "must be greater than or equal to 1."
> Vendor must be registered in Singapore, offer 8h×5-weekday post-sales support and 24/7 email reachability, and have a functional professional website.

Solution categories (https://preapproval-guide.imda.gov.sg/pre-approval-guide/stage-1-vendor-self-assessment/identify-suitable-solution-category): no statistics-monitoring / data-revision category exists; closest fits (Document Management, Data Analytics) have fixed functional checklists this product would not satisfy. Vendor portal: https://services2.imda.gov.sg/VMP (launched 1 Dec 2025). EDGE vendor guide: https://preapproval-guide.imda.gov.sg/edge-grant/edge-grant-vendor-guide (fetched; updated Oct 2026).

**Verdict: not possible today** for a brand-new one-person-company product. Realistic path: acquire 5+ unaffiliated SME customers, keep them 6+ months, document ≥15% productivity gains, incorporate ≥18 months, then apply in a fitting category.

## 6. Market-intelligence / data-purchase schemes

- MRA (closed 29 Sep 2026; https://www.enterprisesg.gov.sg/financial-support/market-readiness-assistance-grant) funded overseas market promotion/BD/set-up only — nothing for domestic data subscriptions. EDGE Internationalisation inherits that scope. LEAD is trade-fair support. No EnterpriseSG programme funds buying a domestic market-intelligence SaaS as such. **Verdict: unlikely.**

---

## Bottom line

**Overall verdict: unlikely today.** (1) The schemes buyers usually cite (PSG/EDG) closed 29 Sep 2026; EDGE is the live route. (2) Every SaaS-subsidy route requires a pre-approved, pre-scoped package in a fixed category — none matches statistics-revision monitoring. (3) Recall Press cannot be pre-approved yet (18-month incorporation + 5 SME references + 15% proven productivity gain). (4) Project grants exclude off-the-shelf subscriptions. **Commercial implication:** sell at full price to research teams and agencies with discretionary budgets; do not build the go-to-market around grant subsidisation. Revisit IMDA pre-approval after ~18 months and 5+ sticky SME customers — that would unlock EDGE's up-to-50% co-pay for future SME buyers.
