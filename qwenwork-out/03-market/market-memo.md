# Market memo — Recall Press, Singapore (one page)

Date: 2026-11-19 · Companion files: prospects.csv (50 orgs), personas.md, pricing-benchmarks.csv, grants-check.md, unit-economics.xlsx, outreach-drafts.md.

**Who pays.** Organisations that republish official statistics on a schedule and get judged on accuracy: (1) property agency research teams (ERA, PropNex, OrangeTee, C&W, Knight Frank) — they publish twice per URA/HDB cycle, flash then final, and the flash-day numbers keep circulating; (2) bank/consultancy macro teams (UOB, OCBC, DBS, Capital Economics, Oxford Economics) — same-day notes on advance estimates feed 4–6 downstream products; (3) federations/chambers (SBF, SCCCI, EuroCham, REDAS) — member newsletters and board papers with stale macro backdrops. Newsletters (DollarsAndSense, Financial Horse) feel the pain daily but pay little. Stat boards are mostly sources, not buyers.

**How much.** Adjacent public prices (checked 2026-11-19): chart/reporting tools US$14–39/user/mo (Power BI, Datawrapper, Tableau), newsletter platforms US$18–199/mo (Ghost, beehiiv), while media monitoring and document governance (Meltwater, Muck Rack, Workiva, AvePoint) are all custom-quote — the category buyers already accept four-figure annual contracts for monitoring. Recommended model: **per publication monitored per month**, three tiers — Starter S$180/mo (3 publications), Growth S$490/mo (10), Enterprise S$1,490/mo (30). Effective S$50–60 per publication sits above chart tools (justified: nightly checks + sourced correction drafts + audit trail) and far below media-monitoring quotes. Unit economics (see workbook; QwenWork credit cost is an input cell, currently placeholder): at 10 credits/check and S$0.02/credit, variable cost ≈ S$7.50/publication/mo, gross margin ≈ 85–88%; with the proposed mix (5 Starter + 3 Growth + 2 Enterprise) MRR is S$5,350 and ~14 mixed-tier customers cover S$300 fixed + S$6,000 founder salary.

**Grants.** Do not build GTM on subsidies. PSG/EDG/MRA ceased 29 Sep 2026; the successor EDGE Grant still requires pre-approved, pre-scoped solutions, and IMDA pre-approval needs 18 months' incorporation + 5 SME customers of 6+ months + proven 15% productivity gain — impossible for a new one-person product today. Verdict: unlikely near-term; revisit after ~18 months with 5 sticky SME references (grants-check.md).

**How to reach the first ten.** Founder-led, document-specific outreach: reference one real publication of theirs (all 50 example URLs verified in prospects.csv), ask for 15 minutes about their revision workflow, then demo on their own flash-vs-final or advance-vs-survey pair. Channels: research-series contact routes, LinkedIn role-based approaches, and one warm federation intro (REDAS/SBF staff move between chambers). Property teams decide in weeks; banks take months — start property, land banks later.
