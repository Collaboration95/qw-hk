## Coverage and release dates

All sources below were opened on **19 November 2026**. The archive follows GDP from Q1 2025 through Q3 2026. Its 13 consecutive quarterly advance estimates and surveys supply eight series: overall GDP, manufacturing, construction and all services-producing industries, each measured year on year and seasonally adjusted quarter on quarter. The latter rates are non-annualised. Labour series use the quarter-end overall unemployment rate and total change in employment in persons. Retail sales, CPI and wholesale sales are outside the live archive.

A vintage is a dated publication of a value. Repeated values in separate releases are retained for replay. Quotes generally give the verbatim numeric cell and a locator identifies the table, row, basis and period. On API detection, `published` is the Singapore date on which this archive first observed the changed figure. `dataset_updated` is the portal's timestamp date and can describe a bulk table update. Earlier agency publication dates are available only where supported by dated releases. The original publication documents are linked for inspection.

## Coverage limitations

The per-series statistics describe the archived periods only. GDP starts at the advance estimate. For labour, “first” means the earliest release captured for that period. A period with a single vintage is counted in coverage and excluded from revision frequency and magnitude. The numerator for revision frequency counts periods whose first and latest values differ. Historical intermediate changes that reverse are counted separately in `ever_revised_periods`. The published cutoffs are illustrative briefing thresholds of 6% for overall year-on-year GDP growth, 5% for year-on-year sector growth, 1% for quarterly growth, 2% for unemployment and 10,000 persons for employment change. The thresholds do not represent government targets.
