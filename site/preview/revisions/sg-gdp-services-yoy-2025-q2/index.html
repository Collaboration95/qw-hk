Did Singapore’s services sector grow in Q2 2025 compared with a year earlier?

In Q2 2025, Singapore’s services sector grew 4.5% from a year earlier, compared with the first archived figure of 4.1% on 14 Jul 2025.

First archived4.1%2025-07-14 · published
Current4.5%2026-08-11 · published

What changes on a sample slide?

A slide quoting 4.1% understates the current figure by 0.4 percentage points.

First to current: up · 0.4 percentage points. The sign stayed the same. The figure stayed on the same side of the “below 5.0%” cutoff.
