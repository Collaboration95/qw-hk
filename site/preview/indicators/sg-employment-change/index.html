Total employment change

It counts the change in total employment in Singapore, in people, from the previous quarter.

How often does it change?

The current figure differs from the first in 5 of 6 comparable quarters. Across those 6 quarters the absolute change averaged about 1,567 people and reached 4,400 people at most.

5 of 6 comparable quarters changed at least once between releases. Each quarter counts once in the average, including a zero change. How corrections work.
