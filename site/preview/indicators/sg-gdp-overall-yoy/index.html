Overall GDP · year on year

It measures the change in real output for Singapore’s economy compared with the same quarter a year earlier.

How often does it change?

The current figure differs from the first in 5 of 6 comparable quarters. Across those 6 quarters the absolute change averaged about 0.95 percentage points and reached 1.8 percentage points at most.

6 of 6 comparable quarters changed at least once between releases. Each quarter counts once in the average, including a zero change. How corrections work.
