Dated evidence

Each release in a record links to the original table or document, identifies the row or chart, credits the agency and gives the access date and terms for that source. The open data licence applies to the data.gov.sg tables. Agency releases have their own terms. Employment counts in thousands are converted to people for comparison.

A release can revise earlier quarters even when its title names a newer quarter. The archive records the period of the figure inside the release. Report a correction with the record and its source.
