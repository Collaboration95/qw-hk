# Ten revisions that changed a briefing

Archive checked 2026-11-19. 60 series–quarter records have multiple readings. 57 changed at least once. 56 still differ from their first figure. 18 crossed zero or a briefing cutoff from first to current. Counting intermediate changes as well brings the crossing count to 21.

Editor’s selection across growth, jobs and unemployment. The order gives priority to reversals and cutoffs that change a headline, then substantial moves and a revision that later reversed. People and percentage points use separate units.
