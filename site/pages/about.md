# About Revisionpedia

Recall Press is a one-person company. Its editor maintains Revisionpedia for people who need to know whether a slide about Singapore’s economy still holds up.

The figures and their dated sources are public. Example slides and briefing sentences are labelled as samples. They illustrate how a revision affects wording.
